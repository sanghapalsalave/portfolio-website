## 🎁 Discount Strategy

### When to Offer Discounts:

✅ **First-time clients** (10% off first project)
✅ **Bulk hours** (15-20% off packages)
✅ **Long-term contracts** (3+ months)
✅ **Referrals** (10% off for both parties)
✅ **Students** (already discounted at ₹99/hour)

### When NOT to Discount:

❌ Client says "I have no budget"
❌ Client wants "exposure" instead of payment
❌ Client is rude or disrespectful
❌ Project scope is unclear
❌ Client has unrealistic timeline
