## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Freelance Development

**For Indian Clients:**

- **Why this works:** Affordable for Indian startups/SMEs while still premium positioning
- **Target clients:** Indian startups, small businesses, entrepreneurs

**For International Clients:**
- **Hourly Rate:** $60/hour
- **Why this works:** Competitive for US/Europe while reflecting your senior experience

## 🎯 Why Dual Pricing Works

### The Math Behind It:

**Indian Tutoring (₹99/hour):**
- 10 students × 4 hours/month = 40 hours
- 40 hours × ₹99 = ₹3,960/month (~$48)
- **High volume, low friction**

**International Tutoring ($45/hour):**
- 3 students × 4 hours/month = 12 hours
- 12 hours × $45 = $540/month
- **Low volume, high value**

**Indian Freelance (₹599/hour):**
- 20 hours/month = ₹11,980 (~$144)
- **Local market, steady income**

**International Freelance ($60/hour):**
- 20 hours/month = $1,200
- **Premium market, premium rates**

**Total Potential Monthly Income:**
- Indian: ₹15,940 (~$192)
- International: $1,740
- **Combined: ~$1,932/month** (₹1,60,000+)

This is **in addition** to your full-time salary!

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't undersell yourself** - Your experience is valuable
2. **Don't work for free** (except first consultation)
3. **Don't take projects without clear scope** - Always get requirements in writing
4. **Don't skip contracts** - Use Upwork/Fiverr initially for protection
5. **Don't promise what you can't deliver** - Under-promise, over-deliver
